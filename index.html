About Dr. Maya Reynolds, PsyD

I’m a licensed clinical psychologist based in Santa Monica, California, offering therapy for adults who feel overwhelmed by anxiety, stress, or the lingering effects of past experiences[cite: 1, 2].

Many of the people I work with are high-achieving, thoughtful, and self-aware—but internally feel exhausted, stuck in overthinking, or emotionally on edge[cite: 2]. Clients frequently come to me feeling “functional” on the outside while quietly struggling with constant worry, tension in their body, difficulty sleeping, or a sense that they’re always bracing for something to go wrong[cite: 2].

I take a warm, collaborative, and grounded approach to therapy[cite: 1, 2]. Sessions are structured enough to feel supportive, while still leaving space for reflection and depth[cite: 2]. I integrate evidence-based methods such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), EMDR, mindfulness-based practices, and body-oriented techniques to help clients understand both the emotional and physiological sides of what they’re experiencing[cite: 1, 2].

Trauma work is an essential pillar of my practice[cite: 2]. I work with adults who have experienced single-incident trauma as well as complex, long-standing patterns stemming from childhood, relationships, or chronic stress[cite: 2]. My approach is paced carefully, emphasizing safety, stabilization, and helping you feel more regulated in daily life—not just during our sessions[cite: 2].

I believe therapy works best when you feel respected, understood, and actively involved[cite: 2]. My goal is not just symptom relief, but helping you cultivate insight, resilience, and a deeper relationship with yourself[cite: 2].

Core Clinical Pillars
